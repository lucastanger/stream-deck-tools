Accessibility setup

  1. Click Request permission. macOS may list Stream Deck, Teams Mic Helper, or the responsible launching app.
  2. Open Accessibility settings and enable the app macOS identifies. You can use Show helper to locate the bundled helper if it must be added manually using the + button.
  3. Join a Teams call. Click Refresh status.

Limitations

Prototype for English-language Teams on macOS. It uses Accessibility, not Microsoft's local API. Teams must expose exactly one matching control for this action. Microphone background operation was tested; minimised windows and future Teams updates may make controls unavailable.

No control found does not prove your microphone or camera is off, or that you are out of a call. Unknown is never treated as off. Accessibility cannot atomically set state: avoid changing the same control elsewhere at exactly the same moment as pressing this key. Pressing the camera key while off will turn it on. Do not use this as a privacy guarantee.
